Savrly, Inc. (“Savrly”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, website, and related services.

01 Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you provide directly to us, information collected automatically through your device, and data from trusted third-party services.

  • Name, email, & phone credentials
  • Profile avatars & taste profile metrics
  • Reviews, pictures, & restaurant ratings
  • Real-time or zip code geolocation
  • Device diagnostic metrics & analytics
  • Standard browser cookie metrics

02 How We Use Your Information

Savrly uses your information to personalize your experience, improve our services, recommend restaurants and events, and provide support.

  • Personalized recommendations
  • Performance optimizations
  • Transaction & marketing alerts
  • Security auditing & safe practices
  • Merchant & user networking features

03 Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and tracking technologies to improve functionality, remember preferences, analyze traffic, and support marketing efforts.

  • Google Analytics Suite
  • Meta Pixel Integrations
  • Newsletter Delivery Metrics

04 Sharing Information

We may share information with trusted service providers, analytics partners, restaurants, event organizers, and authorities when legally required.

🛡️ We strictly do not sell your personal data to third parties.

05 Public Content

Reviews, photos, videos, and other publicly shared content may appear across the platform and may be used by Savrly for promotional or discovery purposes.

06 Your Rights & Choices

You can access, update, or delete your information, manage notifications, and opt out of marketing communications at any time.

07 Data Security

We implement modern security practices including encryption, access controls, and secure infrastructure to protect your information.

08 Children's Privacy

Savrly is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

09 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.
